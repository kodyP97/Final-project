DUNGEON EXPLORER

HOW TO PLAY

You are trapped in a dark dungeon. Find the Treasure Vault, and stay alive.

  • Goal: reach the Treasure Vault, which is always the room farthest from the entrance.
  • Move: use the arrow keys, the N/S/E/W keys, or the on-screen arrows. Press L or the eye button to look around.
  • Map: rooms you visit appear on the map. Dashed “?” tiles are rooms you haven’t seen yet.
  • Danger: some rooms are deadly. Step into one and it’s game over. If you see “You sense danger nearby…”, one of the exits leads to a deadly room.
  • Difficulty: pick Easy, Normal or Hard from the dropdown. Bigger dungeons have more deadly rooms.
  • New dungeon: every game is randomly generated.

YOU WIN!

Congratulations, you escaped with the treasure!
